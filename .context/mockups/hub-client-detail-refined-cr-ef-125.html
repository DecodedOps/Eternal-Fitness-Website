6 · Read-only sources — what the trainer can and cannot do from this list
[PROFILE]
Written here, edited here, deleted here. The delete control appears on hover, as it does today.
[SESSION]
Belongs to that session’s log. Pinnable from this list, but edited and deleted in the session — the row links straight there.
[EXERCISE]
Belongs to that exercise inside that session. Same rule. Deleting a clinical capture from a roll-up view is how history quietly disappears, so this list never offers it.
